# AI usage log

One entry per AI-assisted step: date, tool, what was asked, what was produced, and what a human checked or changed. Used for the AI disclosure in the Space Apps submission.

**Role of the author.** The author is not a mathematician and did not do the calculations by hand. The author directed the work: read and learned about each step, asked the AI to explain what was being done, confirmed the next step, and made the project decisions (method, frame, sites, tolerance, scoring, defaults). The calculations and most of the code were written by AI tools and checked by tests against an independent NASA/JPL SPICE reference.
